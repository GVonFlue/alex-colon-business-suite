THE BUG. 22 BROKEN PILLS ACROSS SIX SCREENS.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

    <Pill color="${BRAND.colors.indigo}">

In JSX that is a PLAIN STRING, not a template literal. Every one of those
pills was handed the eleven characters "${BRAND.co..." as a CSS colour, which
is invalid, so the browser dropped it and the pill fell back to grey.

Twenty-two of them, in Dashboard, Commission, Tools, Books, PCS and Tasks.
That is a real part of why these screens looked washed out: the colour coding
that was supposed to separate overdue from upcoming, won from lost, simply was
not painting. Fixed to color={BRAND.colors.indigo} everywhere.

It builds clean either way, which is why it survived. Nothing errors when you
give an element an invalid colour.

TASKS, REBUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  a filter bar with live counts: Open / Done, then All, Overdue, Today,
    Next 7, No date -- counted over OPEN tasks, because a chip that counts
    finished work is a chip nobody clicks twice
  a FOCUS list, capped at 6, above everything else
  per-row actions: focus, dials, delete
  impact / urgency / effort on manual tasks

FOCUS IS PORTED WHOLE FROM PROYTECH, because the mechanism is better than a
boolean: it stores the DAY it was set rather than true, so it expires on its
own with no nightly job, no timer, and it is still correct if nobody opens the
CRM for three days. The day rolls at 4am so working late does not clear the
list out from under you mid-evening. Picking a task counts once per day
however many times it is toggled.

WHAT I DID NOT PORT, AND WHY. ProyTech scores EVERY task on three dials and
AI-ranks over them. That exists because ProyTech's tasks are freeform and
mostly undated -- something has to decide what comes first.

Half the tasks here are not freeform. Contracts.jsx writes one per contract
deadline, off a real clause, with a real date. The date already IS the
urgency, and asking somebody to score a task the contract scored is work for
its own sake. So the dials appear on MANUAL tasks and never on deadline ones,
and Focus orders each by what is actually known about it: a deadline by how
close its date is, a manual one by impact times urgency.

Deliberately NOT one blended score across both. A contract deadline three days
out and a typed task somebody rated 5 are not comparable quantities, and
pretending they are produces a confident ordering nobody can explain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIVITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

A counts strip with per-kind filtering: All, Calls, Texts, Emails,
Appointments, Notes, Feedback, Tasks, Imports. Ordered by what Alex actually
does rather than alphabetically, and a kind with nothing in it is not rendered
at all -- an empty chip is a dead control.

Counts are computed AFTER the date and person filters but BEFORE the kind
filter, so clicking Calls does not zero the other numbers and leave nothing to
click back to.

A bare reverse-chronological list answers "what happened". The counts answer
"how much of it", which is the question somebody opens this screen with.
