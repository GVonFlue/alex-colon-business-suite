WHY NOTHING CHANGED LAST TIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

src/styles.js in the repo had ZERO of the task CSS in it. There is a commit
called "Update styles.js" that replaced the file, so every class the new markup
used -- .tkr, .tk-sec, .tk-cap, .ac-chips, all of it -- had no rule behind it.

The components shipped. The stylesheet did not. Unstyled markup renders as a
plain stack of divs, which is exactly what you saw, and nothing errors, so
there was nothing to catch it.

All of it is appended at the END of the CSS template now rather than inserted
at an anchor, so a future full-file replacement of styles.js cannot silently
drop it again without the loss being obvious.

WHAT NOW MATCHES PROYTECH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Today / Tomorrow / date quick-pick on the add row
  Three filter segments -- Mine/Everyone, Open/Done/All, and
    Due today / Upcoming / No date / All, each with a live count
  AI RANK, with Clear ranking, the #1 #2 #3 badges and the one-line reason
    under each title

  the "picked 3x" badge
  tap-the-date-chip to reschedule without opening anything
  the overdue banner and the no-date hint under the filter row

FOCUS IS PORTED WHOLE because the mechanism is better than a boolean: it
stores the DAY it was set, so it expires on its own with no nightly job and is
still correct if nobody opens the CRM for three days. Rolls at 4am so working
late does not clear the list mid-evening. Picking counts once per day however
many times it is toggled -- which is what makes "picked 3x" mean anything.

THE SOFT CAP warns at six and never blocks. A cap that refuses you at 4pm when
something urgent lands is how a tool gets abandoned.

"picked 3x" is the most useful thing on the screen. A task pulled into Focus on
three separate days and still open is blocked or badly scoped, and nothing else
in the product surfaces that.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
AI RANKING — NEW ENDPOINT, DIFFERENT PROMPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

api/rank-tasks.js is new. The endpoint is ported; the PROMPT is rewritten.

ProyTech's ranks against "collect $10,000 in cash within 3 weeks", which is
ProyTech's own sprint goal and means nothing to a real estate agent. Copying it
verbatim would have produced confident rankings against somebody else's
business objective, which is worse than no ranking at all.

Alex's ranks against keeping deals alive and leads warm, in this order:
  1. contract deadlines -- off a real clause, usually not recoverable
  2. speed to lead -- an uncontacted lead decays in hours, not days
  3. keeping a live deal moving
  4. work that wins the next deal
  5. everything else by impact x urgency, lower effort breaking ties

It is behind guard() with requireAuth, per-IP limits and `spends: true`, so it
sits inside the SAME daily dollar ceiling as jarvis, ai, extract-contract and
parse-receipt rather than opening a fifth budget.

Hallucinated or duplicate ids are filtered against what was sent, so the worst
case is a partial ranking the screen handles, not a wrong one.

A missing API key returns ok:false and the screen says so plainly and keeps
sorting by impact x urgency. Unconfigured is a setup state, not an error.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ONE DELIBERATE DIFFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

ProyTech scores every task on three dials by hand. Half the tasks here are not
typed by anybody -- Contracts.jsx writes one per contract deadline, off a real
clause, with a real date. The date already IS the urgency.

So the dials are editable on MANUAL tasks and hidden on deadline ones, and the
ranking prompt is told a contract deadline outranks a judgement call. Asking
somebody to rate a task the contract already rated is work for its own sake.

ACTIVITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The ProyTech tile row -- Total logged, Calls, Texts, Emails, Appointments,
Notes -- plus per-kind filter chips. Counts are computed AFTER the date and
person filters and BEFORE the kind filter, so clicking Calls does not zero
every other number and leave nothing to click back to. A kind with nothing in
it is not rendered; an empty chip is a dead control.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
ALSO IN HERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

db.upsertTasks() -- a bulk writer. Ranking rewrites every open task at once,
which is forty round trips one at a time, and forty chances to end up
half-ranked. Same row shape as upsertTask, deliberately: two writers building
the row two different ways is how a column quietly stops being written on one
path.
